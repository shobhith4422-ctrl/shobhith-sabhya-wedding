शिवशक्त्योः दिव्यैक्यम् · The eternal union
शिवः शक्त्या युक्तो यदि भवति शक्तः प्रभवितुंShivah shaktyaa yukto yadi bhavati shaktah prabhavitumUnited with Shakti, Shiva becomes capable of creation.न चेदेवं देवो न खलु कुशलः स्पन्दितुमपि ।Na chedevam devo na khalu kushalah spanditum apiWithout Her, the Divine is not even able to stir.अतस्त्वामाराध्यां हरिहरविरिञ्चादिभिरपिAtas tvaam aaraadhyaam hari-hara-virinchaadibhir apiTherefore, You are worthy of worship by Vishnu, Shiva, Brahma and all the gods.प्रणन्तुं स्तोतुं वा कथमकृतपुण्यः प्रभवति ॥ १॥Pranantum stotum vaa katham akrita-punyah prabhavatiHow could one without spiritual merit bow to You or offer You praise?
Meaning

Lord Shiva becomes capable of creation only when united with Shakti. Without Her, even He cannot move. This verse celebrates the divine power of Shiva and Shakti together.
